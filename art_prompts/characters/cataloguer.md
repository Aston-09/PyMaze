# Cataloguer
**Role:** librarian of the Card Catalogue who teaches dictionaries / key-value lookup (Ch5/Ch5b). Meticulous, hushed, sly.

## Portrait prompt
A slender, precise librarian-archivist in a high-collared charcoal robe with silver key-shaped
brooches, half-moon spectacles, hair in a tight bun. Surrounded by floating index cards and a
wall of tiny labelled drawers; one glowing card hovers at the fingertip mid-lookup, a key of
light in the other hand. Poised, whispering, encyclopedic. Full body, 3/4 view,
flat/transparent background.

## Idle animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, cards still.
- **Frame 2:** floating index cards fan and reshuffle; a drawer glides open glowing.
- **Frame 3:** the key-of-light turns and a matching card lifts out and illuminates.
- **Frame 4:** card slots back, settle (loops to Frame 1).
