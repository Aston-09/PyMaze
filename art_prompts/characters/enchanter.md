# Enchanter
**Role:** final mentor of the Bargain who teaches functions & scope (Ch7). Charismatic, ambiguous, powerful.

## Portrait prompt
A commanding enchanter in ornate flowing robes of deep crimson and gold with high shoulders and
an embroidered spell-sigil sash. Sharp handsome features, a confident half-smile that hides
intent, one hand raised conjuring a floating rune-circle of interlocking glowing glyphs — a spell
being "defined". Rings of light orbit the wrist. Regal, magnetic, a hint of danger in the eyes.
Full body, 3/4 view, flat/transparent background.

## Idle animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, rune-circle steady.
- **Frame 2:** the floating rune-circle rotates and its glyphs light up in sequence; robe hem sways.
- **Frame 3:** he closes his hand and the spell compresses to a bright point, then re-expands.
- **Frame 4:** settle to base (loops to Frame 1).
