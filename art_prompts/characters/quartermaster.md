# Quartermaster
**Role:** caravan supply-master who teaches lists (Ch4/Ch4b). Brisk, organised, good-humoured.

## Portrait prompt
A burly, cheerful caravan quartermaster in a practical brown-and-ochre coat with many buckled
pouches and a wide belt of tallies and tags. Holds a clipboard-scroll and a quill; a floating
row of glowing supply crates lined up neatly beside her like an ordered list. Sturdy boots, a
tallying grin, sleeves rolled. Behind-the-scenes competence and warmth. Full body, 3/4 view,
flat/transparent background.

## Idle animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, crates still, quill down.
- **Frame 2:** the row of glowing crates shuffles — one slides to the end of the line, indices glow.
- **Frame 3:** she ticks the scroll with the quill and nods; a new crate pops into the row.
- **Frame 4:** settle to base (loops to Frame 1).
