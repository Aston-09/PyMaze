# Player  ("You")
**Role:** the summoned traveler — the protagonist. Appears in reflection/silhouette moments.
Keep gender-neutral and lightly customizable so any player sees themselves.

## Portrait prompt
A young summoned traveler of ambiguous origin, wearing simple otherworldly clothes — a plain
tunic and traveling cloak that look out of place in this fantasy world (a hint of modern cut).
Determined but uncertain expression, tousled hair, empty hands ready to learn. Gender-neutral
androgynous design, warm neutral palette, a faint magical summoning glow still clinging to the
outline. Full body, front view, flat/transparent background.

## Idle animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, calm stance.
- **Frame 2:** slow breathing — shoulders rise/fall; the summoning glow on the outline shimmers.
- **Frame 3:** a slight determined nod / shift of stance; cloak edge sways.
- **Frame 4:** settle to base (loops to Frame 1).

--- STYLE: high-resolution pixel art, detailed 16-bit SNES-era JRPG style, crisp clean pixels, vibrant saturated palette, dithered gradients, strong rim lighting, cinematic fantasy character sprite. 1:1.
--- NEGATIVE: blurry, 3D render, realistic photo, text, watermark, signature, UI, extra limbs, deformed hands.
