# Elder Mage  ("Old Man")
**Role:** the elder met at the forest gate who frames the choice (Ch2). Cryptic, kindly, wry.

## Portrait prompt
A weathered old wandering wizard with a long white beard and bushy brows, wearing layered
travel-worn robes in forest green and deep purple with a wide floppy pointed hat. Leans on a
tall gnarled wooden staff topped with a dim glowing stone. Deep laugh-lines, a knowing twinkle,
one eyebrow raised. Warm and grandfatherly but with hidden depth. Full body, 3/4 view,
flat/transparent background.

## Idle animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, staff-stone dim, robe still.
- **Frame 2:** the staff-stone glows warmer and pulses; his beard and robe shift in a breeze.
- **Frame 3:** he shifts weight on the staff and gives a slow knowing nod.
- **Frame 4:** settle to base (loops to Frame 1).
