# Mysterious Figure  ("???" / "Unknown")
**Role:** the hooded stranger who appears at edges of the story (Ch1b, Ch2). Identity concealed.

## Portrait prompt
A tall cloaked figure in a hooded traveling robe, face lost in deep shadow beneath the hood with
only two faint glowing eyes visible. Worn dark-grey and muted-violet fabric with a single brass
clasp, frayed hem, hands hidden in sleeves. A thin wisp of arcane mist curls around the feet.
Still, watchful, ambiguous — could be friend or threat. Standing pose, slight silhouette rim
light. Full body, front view, flat/transparent background.

## Idle animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, cloak still, eyes dim.
- **Frame 2:** cloak hem and mist sway gently; the two eyes brighten faintly.
- **Frame 3:** the figure tilts its head slightly; a colder highlight passes over the cloak.
- **Frame 4:** settle to base (loops to Frame 1).
