# Alchemist
**Role:** keeper of the Scales who teaches numbers & arithmetic (Ch2b). Precise, exacting, playful.

## Portrait prompt
A sharp-eyed alchemist in a leather apron over a fitted teal coat, sleeves rolled, brass goggles
pushed up on the forehead. Surrounded by floating glass vials of glowing coloured liquid and a
delicate set of balance scales hovering at hand, one pan holding a glowing measured droplet.
Fingerless gloves, a belt of tools, a clever confident smirk. Full body, 3/4 view,
flat/transparent background.

## Idle animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, scales level, vials still.
- **Frame 2:** the balance pans tip and re-level; vial liquids bubble and glow brighter.
- **Frame 3:** the alchemist plucks a droplet of light and it rises; a puff of coloured vapor drifts.
- **Frame 4:** settle to base (loops to Frame 1).
