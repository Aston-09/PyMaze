# Celestial Dragon
**Role:** divine arbiter of the first trial (Ch1). Ancient, immense, tests the traveler's resolve.

## Portrait prompt
A colossal celestial dragon, majestic and serene rather than monstrous. Long sinuous serpentine
body with luminous scales shading from deep violet to gold, feathered wings edged with light,
glowing amber eyes, curling horns and a flowing mane of starlight. Wreathed in soft radiant aura
and drifting light particles, coiled mid-air as if descending from the heavens. Regal, awe-
inspiring, benevolent-but-testing expression. Full body, 3/4 view, flat/transparent background.

## Idle animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, wings half-spread, aura soft.
- **Frame 2:** wings lift slightly, aura brightens and pulses; mane strands drift.
- **Frame 3:** a slow breath — chest glows, light particles stream off the scales; eyes flare.
- **Frame 4:** settle to base (loops to Frame 1).
