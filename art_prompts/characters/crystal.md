# Crystal
**Role:** a small sentient light-crystal companion in the Sage's lessons (Ch1b). Curious, bright.

## Portrait prompt
A floating sentient gemstone about the size of a fist, a faceted multi-sided crystal of glowing
teal-and-white light with a single soft glowing core that reads as an "eye". Suspended in the
air, orbited by tiny sparkling motes and a faint magical trail. Cute, friendly, alive — a
personality without a face. Centered, glowing halo, flat/transparent background.

## Idle animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, core glow soft, motes slow.
- **Frame 2:** the crystal bobs gently up; core pulses brighter, motes speed up.
- **Frame 3:** it spins a quarter turn showing new facets; a brief light-flare sparkles off an edge.
- **Frame 4:** bob back down, settle (loops to Frame 1).

--- STYLE: high-resolution pixel art, detailed 16-bit SNES-era JRPG style, crisp clean pixels, vibrant saturated palette, dithered gradients, strong rim lighting, glowing fantasy sprite. 1:1.
--- NEGATIVE: blurry, 3D render, realistic photo, text, watermark, signature, UI, human face.
