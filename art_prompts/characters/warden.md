# Warden
**Role:** guardian of the Endless Bridge & Echoing Halls, master of loops (Ch3/Ch3b). Stern, patient, tireless.

## Portrait prompt
A tall stoic guardian in weathered green-and-grey layered armor over a hooded ranger's cloak,
face partly shadowed with steady glowing eyes. Bears a long staff or halberd planted like a
sentinel, a lantern of cold green flame at the belt. Vines and moss cling to the old armor as if
he has stood watch for ages. Immovable, dutiful, quietly powerful. Full body, front view,
flat/transparent background.

## Idle animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, lantern flame low, cloak still.
- **Frame 2:** the cold green lantern flame flickers and swells; cloak edge sways.
- **Frame 3:** the Warden straightens and grips the staff; eyes glow brighter, a loop of light circles the staff head.
- **Frame 4:** settle to base (loops to Frame 1).
