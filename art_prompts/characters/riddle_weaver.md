# Riddle Weaver
**Role:** night-shrouded riddler at the Loom of Words who teaches strings (Ch6/Ch6b). Cryptic, lyrical, teasing.

## Portrait prompt
An enigmatic weaver seated at a glowing loom under a starlit sky. Flowing deep-violet and midnight
robes stitched with shifting silver thread-glyphs, a partial mask or veil, long fingers trailing
strands of luminous woven text-light between them like thread. Threads of glowing letters spool
from the loom into the air, forming and unforming words. Graceful, mysterious, playful menace.
Full body, 3/4 view, flat/transparent background.

## Idle animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, threads still.
- **Frame 2:** glowing thread-strands drift and weave between the fingers; robe-glyphs shimmer.
- **Frame 3:** the loom pulses and spins new letter-light into the air; the veil flutters.
- **Frame 4:** threads settle (loops to Frame 1).
