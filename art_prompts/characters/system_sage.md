# System Sage
**Role:** the living embodiment of the System (Ch1b). A wise, patient mentor who explains variables.

## Portrait prompt
A dignified old sage, the personification of an arcane operating system. Long silver beard and
robes of deep indigo woven with faintly glowing golden circuit-like runes that pulse slowly.
Kind, knowing eyes; a calm half-smile. Holds a floating open book of light; small holographic
glyphs orbit his hand. Seated or standing at ease. Scholarly, warm, otherworldly — half wizard,
half interface-spirit. Full body, 3/4 view, flat/transparent background.

## Idle animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, runes dim, glyphs still.
- **Frame 2:** robe-runes pulse in a slow wave; orbiting glyphs rotate around his hand.
- **Frame 3:** he gestures gently, the floating book's pages glow and a new glyph forms.
- **Frame 4:** settle to base (loops to Frame 1).
