# Sky of Summons
**Used in:** Ch1b Wisdom of System
**Mood:** transition, being lifted between worlds, revelation

## Base prompt
A vast open sky seen from high above the clouds — the moment of being drawn upward. A radiant
vertical column of summoning light rises through swirling clouds toward a glowing arcane sigil
formed of stars. Streams of glowing runes and light particles spiral along the column.
Dawn-coloured sky, teal to gold, endless and weightless. Dreamlike, transcendent scale.

## Animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, sigil steady.
- **Frame 2:** runes and light particles stream upward along the column faster.
- **Frame 3:** the star-sigil rotates slightly and flares brighter; clouds part around it.
- **Frame 4:** flare eases, particles slow (loops to Frame 1).
