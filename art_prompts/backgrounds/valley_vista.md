# Valley Vista (Day)
**Used in:** Ch2 Trial of Choice, Ch3 Endless Bridge
**Mood:** a fresh start by daylight, a path with choices ahead

## Base prompt
A sweeping sunlit valley seen from a high grassy ridge in early morning. A winding dirt path
leads down from the foreground and forks into the distance among rolling green hills. Bright
blue sky with fluffy cumulus clouds, distant snow-capped mountains, a silver river threading the
valley floor, scattered wildflowers close up. Fresh, hopeful, expansive, warm sunlight, vivid
greens and blues. A crossroads feeling — the path clearly splits.

## Animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, clouds and grass still.
- **Frame 2:** clouds drift slowly right; foreground grass and flowers sway in a breeze.
- **Frame 3:** a flock of tiny birds crosses the sky; light dapples shift over the hills.
- **Frame 4:** breeze eases, birds exit frame (loops to Frame 1).
