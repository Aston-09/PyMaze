# Night Forest
**Used in:** Ch0 Awakening (opening), Ch6 Riddle Weaver, Ch7b Spellbook
**Mood:** disorientation, cold arrival, unfamiliar stars

## Base prompt
A dense pine forest at deep night, viewed from the forest floor looking slightly up through
tall black tree trunks. Wet dark grass in the foreground catching faint starlight. Between the
branches overhead, a sky of unfamiliar constellations in cold blue-white. A thin haze of cold
mist drifting low between the trees. Deep indigo and near-black greens, a lonely eerie
atmosphere, no moon visible, empty and silent.

## Animation frames (keep composition fixed, move only the noted element)
- **Frame 1:** base state, mist settled low, stars steady.
- **Frame 2:** cold mist drifts a few pixels to the right, one distant star twinkles brighter.
- **Frame 3:** a faint blue firefly-glow mote floats up between two trunks; mist thins slightly.
- **Frame 4:** return toward base, mist settling, mote fades out (loops back to Frame 1).
