# Night Valley
**Used in:** Ch0 Awakening, Ch6 Riddle Weaver, Ch7 "Kindled" ending
**Mood:** vastness, being far from home, the wrong sky

## Base prompt
A wide clifftop overlook at night: dark pine trees fall away in the foreground into a huge
valley below. Black jagged mountains stand against a sky the colour of deep water, blue to
violet. A thin crescent moon rests above the peaks — noticeably too small and the wrong shape,
subtly uncanny. A thread of river glints far down in the valley. Sweeping cinematic scale,
cold blues and purples, awe mixed with unease.

## Animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, still sky.
- **Frame 2:** a band of thin cloud drifts slowly across the mountains; river glint shifts.
- **Frame 3:** faint auroral shimmer ripples in the upper sky (teal/violet), moon glow pulses up.
- **Frame 4:** shimmer fades, cloud clears (loops to Frame 1).

--- STYLE: high-resolution pixel art, detailed 16-bit SNES-era JRPG style, crisp clean pixels, vibrant saturated palette, dithered gradients, strong rim lighting, parallax depth, cinematic fantasy atmosphere. 16:9.
--- NEGATIVE: blurry, 3D render, realistic photo, text, letters, watermark, signature, UI, jpeg artifacts.
