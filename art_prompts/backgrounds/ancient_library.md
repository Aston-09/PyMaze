# Ancient Library
**Used in:** Ch1b Wisdom of System, Ch2b Alchemist, Ch5 Grimoire, Ch5b Card Catalogue,
Ch7 Enchanter, Ch7b Spellbook, Ch7c Private Workshop
**Mood:** boundless knowledge, the System's inner sanctum, study and secrets

## Base prompt
The interior of a colossal ancient magical library. Towering bookshelves rise impossibly high
into shadow, packed with worn tomes and scrolls. Warm shafts of golden light fall from tall
arched windows, catching floating dust and a few glowing books drifting gently in mid-air.
Rolling ladders, a large reading table with candles and an open glowing grimoire, ornate carved
wood and brass. Cozy yet vast, amber and deep-green palette, sacred scholarly atmosphere.

## Animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, candles lit, books still.
- **Frame 2:** a floating tome drifts slowly across the room; candle flames flicker.
- **Frame 3:** dust motes swirl in the light shaft; the open grimoire's glow pulses brighter.
- **Frame 4:** tome settles onto a shelf, glow eases (loops to Frame 1).
