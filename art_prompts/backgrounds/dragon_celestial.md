# Celestial Dragon Sky
**Used in:** Ch1 Dragon, Ch1 Dragon Trial, Ch1 Trial Failed, Ch1b Wisdom of System
**Mood:** overwhelming divine presence, the first trial
**Note:** this is the *sky/environment* backdrop; the dragon itself has its own character file.

## Base prompt
An immense sky over the ruins at the moment of a celestial arrival. Towering luminous
cloudscape lit from within, gold and violet, parting around a blazing circular rift of light
high above. Rays of holy light spear down onto distant broken columns below. Distant mountains
on the horizon. Epic, sacred, overwhelming scale — the air itself seems to hum. Leave the upper
sky uncluttered so a large dragon can be composited in.

## Animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, rift steady.
- **Frame 2:** the central rift of light widens and brightens, rays intensifying downward.
- **Frame 3:** clouds swirl inward around the rift; sparks of golden light drift down.
- **Frame 4:** rift settles back to base glow (loops to Frame 1).

--- STYLE: high-resolution pixel art, detailed 16-bit SNES-era JRPG style, crisp clean pixels, vibrant saturated palette, dithered gradients, strong rim lighting, parallax depth, cinematic fantasy atmosphere. 16:9.
--- NEGATIVE: blurry, 3D render, realistic photo, text, letters, watermark, signature, UI, jpeg artifacts.
