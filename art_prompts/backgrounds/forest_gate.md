# Forest Gate  (Sealed Trial Gate)
**Used in:** Ch2 Trial of Choice, Ch3 Endless Bridge
**Mood:** an impassable threshold — no one crosses without passing the trial

## Base prompt
A massive **sealed** stone gateway blocking the only path through the deep forest. Two towering
mossy pillars and a heavy arch frame **closed, iron-bound double doors** that fill the opening
completely — no gap to slip through. Across the doors shimmers a translucent wall of magical
energy (a glowing rune-barrier), and a large central lock-sigil of interlocking glowing runes
holds it shut. Rows of runes run up both pillars and around the arch, lit and active, showing the
seal is **engaged**. The forest path is visible only as a faint glow *behind* the barrier,
tantalisingly out of reach. Ancient, imposing, unmistakably shut. Dappled late-afternoon light,
warm greens and gold against the cold blue-white of the magic seal. Leave ground space in front
for a standing character.

**Key intent:** the gate must read as *locked and impassable* — a wall, not an open archway. The
trial is what opens it.

## Animation frames (barrier stays sealed — this is an idle "locked" loop, not an opening)
- **Frame 1:** base, energy barrier steady, central lock-sigil glowing evenly.
- **Frame 2:** the rune-barrier ripples/shimmers like a curtain of light; pillar runes pulse in sequence.
- **Frame 3:** the central lock-sigil flares brighter and spins slightly, energy crackling at the door seams — reinforcing that it is holding shut.
- **Frame 4:** sigil settles, barrier calms (loops to Frame 1).

> Optional **"unlocked" variant** (generate only if a scene shows the trial passed): same
> composition, doors swung inward, barrier dissolved into fading motes, lock-sigil dark, warm
> light and the open path pouring through the gap.
