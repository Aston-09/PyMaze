# Deep Forest
**Used in:** Ch2 Trial of Choice, Ch3 Endless Bridge, Ch3b Echoing Halls
**Mood:** the harder road, the Warden's domain, tests and loops

## Base prompt
The interior of a deep, old-growth forest at midday but heavily shadowed by a thick canopy.
Massive moss-covered tree trunks recede into green gloom, thin god-rays of light piercing
through the leaves to the fern-covered floor. A faint overgrown path disappears between the
trees. Undergrowth, hanging vines, drifting spores in the light beams. Mysterious, enclosing,
deep emerald and teal palette with warm light shafts. Slightly foreboding.

## Animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, light shafts steady.
- **Frame 2:** glowing spores drift slowly up through the god-rays; ferns sway faintly.
- **Frame 3:** the light shafts flicker as an unseen canopy moves; a distant will-o-wisp glows.
- **Frame 4:** wisp fades, shafts steady (loops to Frame 1).

--- STYLE: high-resolution pixel art, detailed 16-bit SNES-era JRPG style, crisp clean pixels, vibrant saturated palette, dithered gradients, strong rim lighting, parallax depth, cinematic fantasy atmosphere. 16:9.
--- NEGATIVE: blurry, 3D render, realistic photo, text, letters, watermark, signature, UI, jpeg artifacts.
