# Sage's Reading Nook
**Used in:** Ch1b Wisdom of System
**Mood:** intimate mentorship, the first real lesson
**Note:** interior close-up variant of the library; the Sage has his own character file.

## Base prompt
A warm, intimate corner of the great library: a deep reading alcove with a worn armchair, a
low table stacked with open books, a floating candelabra and a single hovering glowing crystal
casting soft light. Shelves close on either side, a small round window showing night sky beyond.
Golden candlelight, cozy shadows, dark wood and deep blue. Quiet, focused, the feeling of a
lesson about to begin. Compose with an empty chair / seat area for a seated character.

## Animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, crystal glow soft.
- **Frame 2:** the hovering crystal bobs gently and brightens; candle flames sway.
- **Frame 3:** a page of an open book turns on its own; faint sparkles drift up from the crystal.
- **Frame 4:** crystal dims to base, page settles (loops to Frame 1).

--- NEGATIVE: blurry, 3D render, realistic photo, text, letters, watermark, signature, UI, jpeg artifacts.
