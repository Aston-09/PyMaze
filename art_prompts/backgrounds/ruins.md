# Ancient Ruins
**Used in:** Ch0 Awakening, Ch1 Dragon, Ch3b Echoing Halls, Ch7 "Hoarder" ending
**Mood:** ancient power, a threshold, something still awake underneath — buried, not decorative

## Base prompt
The excavated entrance of an ancient ruin **half-swallowed by a steep grassy hillside** — thick
earth, roots, and moss visibly overtaking broken stone walls on both sides, as if the hill itself
is slowly reclaiming the structure. A row of **partially collapsed stone archways** lead inward
like the start of a buried hall, some capstones fallen, some still standing under a canopy of
overhanging trees and roots breaking through the masonry. Deep cracks web across the flagstone
floor, and from **inside those cracks** — not painted on the floor, but leaking up from beneath
the stone itself — a faint magical glow pulses, cyan-gold light seeping through fissures like
something ancient still breathing underneath. Long dusk shadows, warm-to-cool twilight gradient
behind the hillside silhouette. Oppressive, buried, dormant-power atmosphere — a threshold you
half-dig into rather than a clean open courtyard.

## Animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, glow dim inside the cracks.
- **Frame 2:** the glow in the fissures brightens and pulses warmer, throwing faint light up the
  cracked walls and earth.
- **Frame 3:** thin glowing motes seep up out of the cracks like breath; a loose stone shifts and
  settles with a puff of dust.
- **Frame 4:** glow recedes back into the cracks, motes fade (loops to Frame 1).
