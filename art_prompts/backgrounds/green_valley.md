# Green Valley Meadow
**Used in:** Ch2 Trial of Choice, Ch2b Alchemist's Scales, Ch4 Quartermaster, Ch4b Satchel
**Mood:** a lived-in waypoint, meeting merchants and craftsfolk

## Base prompt
A lush green meadow in a sheltered valley on a bright afternoon. Tall grass and wildflowers,
a lone gnarled tree with full canopy, a worn cart-track curving through. In the mid-distance a
small stone waypoint — a well or a merchant's tent or scales set on a table under an awning.
Rolling forested hills and blue mountains behind, big soft clouds. Warm, welcoming, pastoral
fantasy, saturated greens and gold-hour light. Leave clear ground space for characters/props.

## Animation frames
- **Frame 1:** base, calm.
- **Frame 2:** tall grass and the tree's leaves ripple in a gentle wind; awning cloth flutters.
- **Frame 3:** butterflies or pollen motes drift across the foreground; a cloud shadow slides past.
- **Frame 4:** wind settles (loops to Frame 1).

--- STYLE: high-resolution pixel art, detailed 16-bit SNES-era JRPG style, crisp clean pixels, vibrant saturated palette, dithered gradients, strong rim lighting, parallax depth, cinematic fantasy atmosphere. 16:9.
--- NEGATIVE: blurry, 3D render, realistic photo, text, letters, watermark, signature, UI, jpeg artifacts.
